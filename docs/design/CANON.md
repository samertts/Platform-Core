# Platform-Core Canon

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Canonical

---

## Purpose

The Canon defines the immutable philosophy of Platform-Core.

It explains why the project exists and what it will never compromise.

---

# Canon I

Knowledge is the foundation of every reliable system.

---

# Canon II

Architecture is more important than implementation.

Implementation changes.

Architecture survives.

---

# Canon III

Automation exists to amplify human engineering.

Never to replace human responsibility.

---

# Canon IV

Engineering decisions require evidence.

Not popularity.

Not assumptions.

---

# Canon V

Security is a design property.

Not a feature.

---

# Canon VI

Documentation is part of engineering.

Undocumented work is unfinished work.

---

# Canon VII

Every improvement must increase long-term value.

Never optimize only for short-term speed.

---

# Canon VIII

Everything should be measurable.

If it cannot be measured,
it cannot be improved.

---

# Canon IX

Platform-Core shall remain independent.

No vendor lock-in.

No mandatory cloud.

No mandatory AI provider.

---

# Canon X

Knowledge accumulated by the platform is its greatest asset.

Protect it.

Preserve it.

Improve it.

---

# Canon XI

Builders create systems.

Knowledge creates builders.

Therefore knowledge creates systems.

---

# Canon XII

The ultimate objective of Platform-Core is not software.

It is reliable engineering.

---

End of Canon
