# Platform-Core Founding Charter

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Ratified

Author: Samer Abbas Al-Karawi (Founder)

---

# Mission

Platform-Core exists to transform knowledge into trusted systems.

The platform is designed to build, improve, and sustain reliable software systems through knowledge engineering, automation, governance, and reusable capabilities.

---

# Vision

To become the world's leading open engineering platform for transforming human knowledge into reliable, secure, maintainable and reusable systems.

---

# Core Principles

1. Knowledge Before Code.
2. Architecture Before Implementation.
3. Contracts Before Communication.
4. Security By Design.
5. Automation Before Repetition.
6. Evidence Before Opinion.
7. Human Oversight.
8. Replace Everything.
9. Measure Everything.
10. Improve Forever.

---

# Primary Goals

- Reduce software engineering complexity.
- Build reusable engineering knowledge.
- Create a self-improving engineering platform.
- Preserve engineering knowledge for future generations.
- Remain independent from any single AI provider.

---

# Success Metrics

- Faster engineering.
- Higher quality.
- Better security.
- Lower maintenance cost.
- Greater knowledge reuse.

---

# Motto

Transforming Knowledge into Trusted Systems.
