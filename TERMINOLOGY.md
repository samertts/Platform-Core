# Platform-Core Terminology

## Engine

A long-running intelligent subsystem.

## Builder

Generates engineering assets.

## Factory

Produces Builders.

## Capability

A reusable engineering function.

## Contract

Defines communication rules.

## Manifest

Metadata describing an asset.

## Registry

Stores discoverable assets.

## Runtime

Executes platform components.

## Kernel

The minimal platform core.

## Forge

The engineering factory.

## Observatory

Monitors external engineering ecosystems.

## Laboratory

Experimental environment.

## Knowledge

Validated engineering information.

## Asset

Anything valuable produced by the platform.
